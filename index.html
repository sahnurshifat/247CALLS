Questions

Do I need a contract?

No. You can stop at any time.

How do I get my messages?

By email, with an optional SMS summary after each call.

How fast can I go live?

Usually the same day, once your calls are diverted.

What is the difference between the AI and a real person?

The AI receptionist answers instantly, around the clock, at the lowest cost. A real person gives every caller a human voice, and a Dedicated Receptionist is one named person who learns your business.

Will you answer in my company name?

Yes. Every call is answered using your business name and your own greeting.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing number and simply divert it to the line we give you.

What happens if a call is urgent?

With the Dedicated Receptionist we can transfer urgent calls straight to you. On other packages, urgent messages are sent to you immediately by SMS or email.

How does billing work?

Monthly packages are billed monthly, and per-call packages are billed for the calls answered. All prices exclude VAT.

Can I change package later?

Yes. Just tell us and we will switch you to a package that fits better.
